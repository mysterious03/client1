Who We Are ——

Built On Practical Engineering & Uncompromised Precision.

Based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, Dhanasree Hydraulics & Equipments was founded with a clear, focused mandate: to provide heavy manufacturing and infrastructure enterprises with fluid-power systems that perform reliably under punishing, continuous duty cycles.

Industrial hydraulics leave zero margin for error. A micro-leak in a 350-bar line, a misaligned guide bushing in a four-column press, or an uneven seal on an automotive elevator can halt an entire plant. That is why every system leaving our factory is designed with structural over-capacity, premium alloys, induction-hardened chrome plating, and precision-honed barrels.

Today, our dual presence in Melayanambakkam (Manufacturing Plant) and Padi (Operations & Logistics Center) allows us to serve tier-1 clients across automotive, infrastructure, heavy metal forming, and intra-logistics with unparalleled speed and technical depth.

Quality Assurance Protocols ——

The Engineering Standard

Every product engineered at Dhanasree Hydraulics adheres to rigorous quality control measures from raw material spectroscopic validation to hydrostatic burst integrity.

01

CAD & FEA Stress Analysis

Finite Element Analysis (FEA) is conducted on all cylinder mountings, tie-rods, clevis pins, and press platens to eliminate stress concentrations before metal cutting begins.

02

Hard Chrome Plating Integrity

All piston rods utilize EN8/EN9 alloy steel with 25–30µm hard chrome deposit, surface-ground and polished to Ra < 0.2µm for maximum seal longevity under harsh grit.

03

100% Hydrostatic Proof Testing

Each manufactured power unit and cylinder is subject to continuous 1.5× rated pressure holding cycles with zero-leakage velocity fuses and digital transducer verification.
