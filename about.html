Dhanasree Hydraulics & Equipments manufactures and supplies complete engineering systems and hydraulic power units to the complete satisfaction of our customers at Padi, Chennai.

Who We Are, What We Do

Industrial Engineering Grounded in Precision

Based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, Dhanasree Hydraulics & Equipments is an engineering powerhouse specializing in the design, fabrication, assembly, and turnkey commissioning of heavy-duty hydraulic cylinders, power packs, industrial presses, and lifting equipment.

Our engineering team focuses on real-world industrial durability. Every cylinder rod is machined to exact tolerances, every power pack manifold undergoes rigorous pressure-testing, and each structural weld is inspected for continuous cyclic load endurance.
