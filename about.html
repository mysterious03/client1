Quality Assurance Protocols ——

The Engineering Standard

Every product engineered at Dhanasree Hydraulics adheres to rigorous quality control measures from raw material spectroscopic validation to hydrostatic burst integrity.

01

CAD & FEA Stress Analysis

Finite Element Analysis (FEA) is conducted on all cylinder mountings, tie-rods, clevis pins, and press platens to eliminate stress concentrations before metal cutting begins.

02

Hard Chrome Plating Integrity

All piston rods utilize EN8/EN9 alloy steel with 25–30µm hard chrome deposit, surface-ground and polished to Ra < 0.2µm for maximum seal longevity under harsh grit.

03

100% Hydrostatic Proof Testing

Each manufactured power unit and cylinder is subject to continuous 1.5× rated pressure holding cycles with zero-leakage velocity fuses and digital transducer verification.
